How it works

  1. Your browserstreams microphone audio and plays the avatar over WebRTC
  2. Azure Voice Livespeech recognition, turn detection, neural voice and avatar
  3. Microsoft Foundry agentgpt-realtime with the “Sam” persona and guardrails
  4. MCP server11 tools: verify identity, policies, coverage, billing, claims, callbacks
  5. Insurance APIFastAPI on Azure Container Apps, traced in Application Insights
