How it works

  1. Your browserstreams your microphone and plays Sam's avatar video and voice over WebRTC
  2. Secure relaysigns in to Azure with a managed identity, so no keys reach the browser, and caps call length and volume
  3. Foundry voice agentgpt-realtime with the “Sam” persona, an HD neural voice, semantic turn detection and avatar
  4. MCP server11 tools: verify identity, policies, coverage, billing, claims, callbacks
  5. Insurance APIFastAPI on Azure Container Apps, traced in Application Insights
